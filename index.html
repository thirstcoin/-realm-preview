Ecosystem philosophy

When the Zillaz grow, Toezlandia grows with them

$DAZILLA was not created to replace $TOEZ. Its purpose is to expand the world around it. A portion of future $DAZILLA creator rewards may be used for Toezlandia development, Guardian events, community experiences and ecosystem initiatives.

No guaranteed returns. No promise of profit. No promise of price support. The goal is simple: Zilla activity should create more activity inside Toezlandia.

Every major success of $DAZILLA should create another reason to discover, hold, use or participate in $TOEZ.Not another reason to abandon it.
